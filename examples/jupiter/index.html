The clouds ride Jupiter's jet streams, measured by Voyager, Hubble, Cassini and New Horizons (the lime curve in the Map view: east is right). Storms spin in the bands between the jets. Drag to turn the planet, right-drag or Shift-drag to move it, scroll to zoom, click to start a storm of your own. Keys: Space pauses, [ and ] change speed, comma and full stop jump an hour or a day.
A kinematic sketch, not a fluid simulation: the jets are fixed from observation and the storms are imposed. Jets: average of Voyager, Hubble, Cassini and New Horizons, from John Rogers' table for the BAA Jupiter Section. Great Red Spot width: Parisi et al. (2021), from Juno.
