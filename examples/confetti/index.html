Confetti on a page that keeps working

This is plain HTML. The confetti is one spryte canvas laid over it with pointer-events: none, so you can still select this text, type in the box and press every button while pieces fall through it.

One draw callEvery piece goes through a single sprites() call with its own angle, scale and colour.
Sharp on any screenThe canvas renders at the device pixel ratio, so pieces stay crisp on phones.
Smallspryte is a few kilobytes. Click anywhere for a burst where you click.
